Jiaozi (Chinese Pork & Cabbage Dumplings)

Home

Makes about 45-50 pieces

Dough

  • 500 g all-purpose flour
  • 250–260 ml warm water
  • 1/4 tbsp salt

Filling

  • 500 g ground pork
  • 400 g napa cabbage, finely chopped
  • 2 scallions, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp salt, plus more for salting cabbage
  • ½ tsp white pepper
  • 1 egg
  • 3–4 tbsp water

Dipping Sauce

  • 3 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp black vinegar
  • 1 tsp chili oil
  • 1 small garlic clove, grated
  • A few drops sesame oil

Instructions

  1. Mix flour and salt. Add water gradually and knead into a smooth, firm dough, about 8–10 minutes. Cover and rest for 30–45 minutes.
  2. Toss chopped cabbage with 1 tsp salt. Let sit for 15 minutes, then squeeze out excess water thoroughly.
  3. In a bowl, mix pork, scallions, ginger, soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, sesame oil, salt, white pepper, and egg. Stir in one direction. Add water 1 tbsp at a time until the filling looks moist but not runny. Fold in the squeezed cabbage.
  4. Divide dough into 2–3 logs, cut into small pieces, and roll each into a thin circle about 8–9 cm wide. Keep unused dough covered.
  5. Place about 1 tbsp filling in the center. Fold in half and pinch the center. Pleat one side toward the center, then the other, sealing tightly. Set on a floured tray.
  6. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Add dumplings in batches, stirring gently to prevent sticking. When the water returns to a boil, add ½ cup cold water. Repeat 2–3 times until dumplings float and are cooked through, about 6–8 minutes total. Remove with a slotted spoon.
  7. Serve: Mix dipping sauce ingredients and serve with hot dumplings.

Tips

  • Do not overfill wrappers, or they will burst.
  • Freeze uncooked dumplings on a floured tray, then transfer to bags; cook from frozen.
  • For pan-fried dumplings: fry flat-side down in oil until golden, add ½ cup water, cover, and steam until absorbed.
