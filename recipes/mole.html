Mexican Mole

Home

Serves 6

Ingredients

For the chicken

  • 1 whole chicken, about 1.8 kg, cut into pieces, or 6 chicken thighs
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 onion, halved
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • Water, for simmering

For the mole

  • 4 dried mulato chiles, stemmed and seeded
  • 3 dried ancho chiles, stemmed and seeded
  • 2 dried pasilla chiles, stemmed and seeded
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1/4 cup almonds
  • 1/4 cup peanuts
  • 2 tbsp raisins
  • 2 corn tortillas, torn into pieces
  • 1 slice bolillo or French bread
  • 2 tbsp lard or vegetable oil, plus more as needed
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 3 ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 tomatillos, husked and chopped
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 2 whole cloves
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 cups chicken broth, plus more as needed
  • 40 g Mexican chocolate, chopped, or dark chocolate plus 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 to 2 tsp sugar, or to taste
  • Salt, to taste

To serve

  • Cooked white rice
  • Warm corn or flour tortillas
  • Extra sesame seeds, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Put the chicken in a large pot with the salt, halved onion, and garlic. Add enough water to cover. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 30 to 40 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through. Reserve 2 cups of the cooking broth and keep the chicken warm.
  2. Toast the dried chiles in a dry skillet over medium heat for about 20 to 30 seconds per side, until fragrant but not burned. Transfer to a bowl, cover with hot water, and soak for 20 minutes.
  3. In the same skillet, lightly toast the sesame seeds, almonds, and peanuts separately until fragrant. Set aside. Briefly toast the raisins, then set aside.
  4. Heat 2 tbsp lard or oil in the skillet. Fry the torn tortillas and bread slice until golden and crisp. Remove and set aside.
  5. Add the chopped onion and garlic to the skillet and cook until softened. Add the tomatoes and tomatillos and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, until softened and slightly darkened.
  6. Drain the soaked chiles. Blend the chiles, toasted nuts and seeds, raisins, fried tortillas and bread, cooked onion-tomato mixture, cinnamon, cloves, black pepper, cumin, oregano, and 1 cup of the reserved chicken broth until very smooth. Work in batches if needed, adding more broth to keep the blender moving.
  7. Heat 2 tbsp lard or oil in a large pot over medium heat. Pour in the blended mole paste and cook, stirring often, for about 10 minutes. Add the remaining reserved broth and simmer for 20 to 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  8. Add the chocolate and stir until melted. Season with sugar and salt to taste. Add the cooked chicken and simmer for 10 minutes more, until heated through. If the mole is too thick, loosen it with a little extra broth.
  9. Serve hot with rice and warm tortillas, garnished with sesame seeds.

Tips

  • Use gloves when handling dried chiles, and avoid touching your face afterward.
  • Do not burn the chiles or the mole will taste bitter.
  • Blend the mole in batches for the smoothest sauce.
  • Mole improves in flavor after resting; it can be refrigerated for several days or frozen for later use.
