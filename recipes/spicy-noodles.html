Spicy Noodles

Home

Serves 2

Ingredients

  • 200 g wheat noodles
  • 2 tbsp light soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp chili oil
  • 1 tsp chili flakes
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 scallions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground Sichuan peppercorn, optional
  • 100 g ground pork or chopped mushrooms, optional
  • 1 small bok choy, chopped, optional
  • 2 tbsp noodle cooking water

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Cook the noodles according to the package instructions. Add the bok choy during the last minute if using. Reserve 2 tbsp of the cooking water, then drain.
  2. In a small bowl, mix the soy sauce, chili oil, chili flakes, sesame oil, rice vinegar, sugar, and Sichuan peppercorn if using.
  3. Heat a pan over medium heat. Add the ground pork or mushrooms if using and cook until browned. Add the garlic, ginger, and most of the scallions and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add the cooked noodles and sauce to the pan. Toss well, adding the reserved noodle water a little at a time to help the sauce coat the noodles.
  5. Taste and adjust with more soy sauce, chili oil, or vinegar if needed.
  6. Serve hot, topped with the remaining scallions.

Tips

  • Use more chili oil or chili flakes for a hotter dish.
  • Add a fried egg on top for extra protein.
  • If the sauce is too thick, loosen it with a splash of noodle cooking water.
